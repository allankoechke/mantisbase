bool mb::MbMailer::send ( const MbEmailMsg &  message)

Deliver a message. Mock hosts (mock, localhost:0) capture it in sentMessages() and do not connect. Otherwise the message is sent over SMTP on a trantor TCP connection. Throws MbException on validation errors, rate limits, or delivery failure.
